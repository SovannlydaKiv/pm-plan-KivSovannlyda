# Priority and Veto Stakeholders

The Head of the IT department (S01) and the Data Protection Officer (S08) require the PM's attention first because they both have high influence over the project's survival. The Head of IT controls the budget and ultimate acceptance of the platform, while the Data Protection Officer ensures legal and institutional compliance. 

The Data Protection Officer (S08) is the "veto" stakeholder. Even though their overall day-to-day interest in the app's features is low, they have the absolute power to stop the project or block the Week 14 pilot entirely if the platform violates student data privacy policies.
